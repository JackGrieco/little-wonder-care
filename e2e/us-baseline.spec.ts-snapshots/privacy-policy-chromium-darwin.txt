This policy explains what information I collect when you use Little Wonder Care's website or services, how I use it, and your rights around it. I've written it in plain English because legal jargon helps no one. Information I Collect I only collect information you actively give me. This includes: Contact forms — your name, email address, and any message you send through this site. Booking forms — your name, email, phone number, due date, and any details you share when requesting a service or scheduling a call. Email sign-ups — your email address if you subscribe to receive guides, tips, or newsletters. I don't collect any information without your knowledge, and I never buy or sell contact lists. How I Use Your Information The information you share with me is used to: Respond to your questions and inquiries Deliver the services you've booked or requested Send you resources, guides, or updates you've signed up for Improve this website and my offerings over time I will never sell your personal information to third parties, and I only share it with service providers who help me run my business (see below). Third-Party Services I use a small number of trusted third-party tools to run this website and my services. Each of these processes your data according to their own privacy policies, which I encourage you to review: Formspree — handles contact form submissions and routes messages to my inbox. Cal.com — powers the scheduling and booking system for consultations and discovery calls. Stripe — processes payments securely. I never see or store your payment card details. Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — manages email subscribers and sends newsletters or guides you've opted into. Analytics — I may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Vercel Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. No personally identifiable data is collected for this purpose. Cookies This site uses minimal cookies. I do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies for cross-site behavioral profiling. Any cookies in use are either essential for the site to function or used for basic, anonymized analytics to understand how the site is being used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use this site. Data Security I take reasonable measures to protect your information. This site is served over HTTPS (SSL encryption), and the third-party services I use (Stripe, Cal.com, Kit) are industry-standard platforms with their own robust security practices. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but I'm committed to handling your data responsibly and only keeping it as long as necessary. Children's Privacy My services are designed to support parents and families — I absolutely love working with families who have little ones! However, this website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, please contact me and I'll remove it promptly. Your Rights You have the right to: Access the personal information I hold about you Correct any information that is inaccurate Request deletion of your personal information Unsubscribe from email communications at any time (every email includes an unsubscribe link) To exercise any of these rights, just send me an email at silvia@littlewondercareco.com and I'll take care of it.
